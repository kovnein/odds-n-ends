How to play SPILL

  • Draw hand & start — set your options in the gear menu, then draw your starting tiles.
  • Drag tiles — from tray to felt, or rearrange freely on the felt.
  • Select multiple — click tiles to select them (they'll glow gold), then drag any of them to move the whole group together. Click empty space to clear the selection.
  • ↻ Rotate — select 2 or more placed tiles, then rotate that shape 90° in place.
  • Right-click and hold on the felt to pan around the grid.
  • ⏸ Pause — freezes the spill countdown; you can keep rearranging while paused.
  • 🎲 Draw now — get your next spill of tiles immediately instead of waiting.
  • ♻ Dump 1→3 — select one tray tile and trade it for three fresh ones. Off-limits once only 3 tiles remain in the bag.
  • 📖 Dictionary — toggle word-checking on/off, and choose between the NZ dictionary (English + common te reo words like kia ora, whānau, kai) or a smaller common-words-only list.
  • 💾 Share & save — copy a text snapshot with a board code, or paste a code to load any saved or shared board.
  • Tile pool (in settings) — shrink the bag to simulate a shorter game for 2–8 players.
